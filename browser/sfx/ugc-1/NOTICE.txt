Modisign sound pack ugc-1
=========================

This folder holds ten short sound effects used by Modisign Studio and the
Modisign MCP edit_video tool. They are third-party recordings, not Modisign
designs. Each one was published by its author or publisher under CC0 1.0
Universal (public domain dedication). The legal code is in licenses/CC0-1.0.txt.

CC0 requires no credit. The lines below are given voluntarily, so you can find
the sources. Sound names are the names Modisign shows.

What was changed
----------------

Each sound was decoded and converted to 16-bit stereo WAV at 48 000 Hz (mono
sources are duplicated to both channels) and had its leading silence trimmed
(and, for ugc-sparkle-01, a long silent tail). No gain, loudness
normalization, filter or effect was applied. manifest.json records the exact
conversion command, trimmed frame counts and SHA-256 of each source and each
distributed file.

Where to find the data
----------------------

manifest.json in this folder lists every file, its source, its licence
evidence, its hashes and its conversion. Files are named
<id>.<first 8 hex characters of the SHA-256>.wav, so a changed file always has
a new name.
